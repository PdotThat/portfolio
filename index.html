Hello! My name is Tangmo. I am a Computer Science student specializing in Data Science and Cybersecurity, with a strong interest in the field of cyber security. I am still exploring the path where I can grow in this industry, and I am excited to keep learning and developing in these areas.

My cyber security background is still at a foundation level — coursework, CTF competitions, and hands-on labs.
